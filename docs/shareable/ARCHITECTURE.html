GuardBee Architecture Overview

ComponentResponsibility
app.pyStreamlit UI, state, and safe flow coordination
data_loader.pyJSON parsing and validation
engine.pyDeterministic risk and evidence
retrieval.pyTF-IDF policy retrieval with a 0.08 threshold
ai_client.pyOptional Nebius client and citation-ID gate
workflow.pyContext fingerprint and one-decision lock

Safety boundary

Risk is deterministic. AI is optional and cannot enable a decision without current event/policy IDs. Outcomes are session-only simulations; GuardBee cannot modify a real account or system.
